# 🚌 SmartCommute

### Your smarter route. Your greener journey.

SmartCommute is a student-focused mobile application designed to help users choose better transportation options based on travel time, cost, and environmental impact.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students often face difficulties choosing the best transportation option for their daily commute. They may spend extra time and money due to traffic and may not know which option is more environmentally friendly.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

SmartCommute compares different transportation options such as bus, car, cycle, and walking based on:

- ⏱️ Travel Time
- 💰 Estimated Cost
- 🌱 Eco Score
- 📍 Route Information

## ✨ Key Features

- Smart Route Search
- Transport Preference Selection
- Route Comparison
- Travel Time Comparison
- Cost Comparison
- Eco Score
- Journey Tracking
- Carpool Support
- Eco Points
